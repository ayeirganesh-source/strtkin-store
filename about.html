Our Story

Architectural Minimalist Streetwear

STRTKIN is an independent streetwear label born from architectural minimalism, heavyweight textiles, and raw counter-culture design language.

Founded in India, our mission is to eliminate cheap, fast-fashion silhouettes in favor of structured 240+ GSM organic cottons, dropped-shoulder boxy fits, and industrial hardware crafted to last a lifetime.

Every collection is engineered in limited runs—once a drop sells out, it is never reproduced in the exact same colorway.
